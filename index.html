02 — The Atelier

Made by hand,not by haste.

Every Saksham piece begins on a wooden loom in Rajasthan. Our weavers set the warp by hand, one thread at a time, before a single metre of cloth exists. Nothing is rushed; the loom decides the pace.

Meet the makers
03 — The Vat

Colour thatcomes from earth.

Indigo, madder root, pomegranate rind. Our dyes are fermented in open vats and fixed with sunlight, so each garment carries a slightly different depth — a fingerprint of the day it was made.

See the palette
04 — The Print

Patterns pressedby human hands.

Our block prints are carved from teak and stamped one impression at a time. Small misalignments are not flaws — they are the signature of a person, not a machine.

06 — Our Story

Since 2014, clothing that awakens stories.

Saksham began in a two-room studio in Jaipur with one loom, two weavers and a stubborn belief that clothes should be made slowly. Today the studio works with forty artisans across Rajasthan — and still keeps the first loom by the door.

07 — From fibre to form

The journey ofa garment.

Origin

It starts in the field.

We buy cotton and silk directly from growers in Gujarat and Bihar, paying above market so the fibre can be grown slowly and picked by hand.

  • Direct trade
  • Rain-fed cotton
  • No middlemen
Weave

The loom sets the pace.

A single metre of our handloom takes a weaver a full day. The slight irregularity you can feel is the proof that a person, not a machine, made it.

  • Wooden pit looms
  • 40 artisans
  • 1 metre / day
Dye

Colour from the ground.

Indigo, madder and pomegranate are fermented in open vats. Every batch shifts with the weather, which is why no two Saksham pieces are identical.

  • Natural dyes
  • Sun-fixed
  • Zero synthetic colour
Wear

Then it becomes yours.

We repair any Saksham garment for life. Bring it back faded, mended or worn thin — we will patch it, re-dye it, and send it home again.

  • Lifetime repairs
  • Free alterations
  • Take-back programme
